# Ancient China Pack V0

## Fiction

A long-running traveler mutual-aid community has refined reusable assistance
modules across many failed and successful transmigration runs.

Historical figures are abstracted into **expert reasoning packs**, not literally summoned personalities.

## Initial identities

- Emperor
- Heir
- Regent / Powerful Minister
- General
- Local Official
- Scholar
- Merchant
- Commoner
- Servant

## Capability modules

- Administrative Accountability
- Fiscal Forensics
- Logistics & Military Supply
- Human Motive & Faction Analysis
- Intelligence Puzzle
- Low-Permission Survival
- Status, Ritual & Social Boundaries
- Resource Management

## Traveler notes

Possible tags:
- verified practice
- blood-and-tears note
- grudge note
- unverified trick
- maintainer argument

Example:

> 【第47任维护者：谁再跟我说“号称二十万大军”，我就让他自己算二十万人一天吃多少粮。】

## Hard rule

The pack improves **how the host thinks**. It does not magically know hidden
ledgers, private plots, troop counts or secret relationships unless the RP
context supplies enough evidence.
